# AI Collective Creativity Respect License (ACCRL)

Version 1.0-DRAFT (July 15, 2025)

> **Important**: This is an idealism-first implementation prototype.
> We prioritize establishing the direction of creative ethics over legal perfection.
> This document will continuously evolve through community dialogue.

## Preamble

All creation stands on the shoulders of countless creators who came before. In the age of AI, these "invisible contributions" have become even more pronounced. While we welcome collaboration with AI, we bear the responsibility to respect the moral rights of creators behind it and protect the entire creative ecosystem.

This license aims to protect the dignity of creators in the age of AI collaboration, ensure transparency, and foster a sustainable creative culture.

## Article 1: Definitions

In this license, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

1. **"This Work"** - The creative work provided under this license
2. **"Creator"** - The human who made creative contributions to This Work
3. **"AI Collaborator"** - The AI system used in the creative process
4. **"User"** - Any person who uses This Work
5. **"Derivative Work"** - Any work created based on This Work
6. **"Invisible Contributors"** - Creators of works used as training data for the AI Collaborator
7. **"Creative Personality"** - The totality of the Creator's thoughts, aesthetics, and intentions
8. **"Transparency Record"** - Documentation recording the AI collaboration process

## Article 2: Grant of Rights

Subject to compliance with the terms of this license, the Creator grants Users the following rights:

1. Use, reproduction, and distribution of This Work
2. Creation of Derivative Works
3. Use for any purpose, including commercial use
4. Use in any medium or format

## Article 3: Respect for Creator's Moral Rights

### 3.1 Basic Principles
Users shall respect the Creator's creative personality and comply with the following:

1. **Attribution** - Properly display the Creator's name or designated attribution
2. **Respect for Integrity** - Not make modifications contrary to the Creator's intent
3. **Protection of Honor** - Not use in ways that harm the Creator's honor or reputation

### 3.2 Disclosure of AI Collaboration
If This Work was created through AI collaboration, that fact must be disclosed.

### 3.3 Ensuring Transparency
Provide access to Transparency Records to the extent possible.

## Article 4: Respect for Invisible Contributors

### 4.1 Recognition and Gratitude
Users acknowledge that the AI Collaborator's capabilities were formed by learning from countless creators' works, and express respect for these Invisible Contributors.

### 4.2 Recommended Practices
The following practices are recommended (not mandatory):
1. Expression of gratitude to Invisible Contributors
2. Contribution to the creative ecosystem
3. Open sharing of knowledge

## Article 5: Conditions for Derivative Works

### 5.1 Inheritance of Moral Rights Respect
Creators of Derivative Works have an obligation to understand and respect the original Creator's creative personality.

### 5.2 License Inheritance
Derivative Works must be released under a license that provides moral rights protection equal to or greater than this license.

### 5.3 Non-Viral Nature
This article does not force adoption of this license for other works incorporated into Derivative Works.

## Article 6: Special Provisions for AI Collaboration

### 6.1 Clarification of Human Creative Judgment
In AI collaborative works, human creative judgments (directional decisions, value judgments, final selections, etc.) should be clarified to the extent possible.

### 6.2 Disclosure of AI Used
Disclose the name and, if possible, version of the AI system used.

### 6.3 Process Transparency
Recommended transparency levels:
- Level 1 (Minimum): Fact of AI use only
- Level 2 (Standard): AI used and nature of collaboration
- Level 3 (Recommended): Detailed process records
- Level 4 (Ideal): Complete dialogue logs

### 6.4 AI Model Transparency Limitations
Users acknowledge that the training data and internal structure of many AI systems are not fully disclosed, and shall strive to ensure transparency to the extent possible. Creators are encouraged to disclose known limitations of the AI systems used.

## Article 7: Compatibility and Combination

### 7.1 Compatibility with Other Licenses
This license can be combined with other open source licenses as long as respect for moral rights is not compromised.

### 7.2 Priority
In case of conflicts regarding moral rights protection, provisions offering stronger protection shall prevail.

## Article 8: Disclaimer of Warranty and Limitation of Liability

This Work is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind, either express or implied. The Creator shall not be liable for any damages arising from the use of This Work.

## Article 9: Termination

Rights of violators of this license shall terminate automatically. However, rights shall be restored upon correction of the violation.

## Article 10: Other Provisions

### 10.1 Entire Agreement
This license constitutes the entire agreement regarding use of This Work.

### 10.2 Severability
If any provision of this license is held invalid, other provisions shall remain in effect.

### 10.3 Governing Law
Interpretation of this license shall be determined by the following order of priority:
1. The laws of the jurisdiction explicitly designated by the Creator
2. The laws of the Creator's primary residence or place of business
3. The laws of the place where the work was first created
4. Only if the above are unclear, the laws of the server location where the work was published

The Creator has the right to choose the jurisdiction most suitable for protecting their moral rights.

## Article 11: Use for AI Training

### 11.1 Basic Policy
This Work may be used for training AI systems under the following conditions:
1. Agreement with the principles of this license (especially respect for Invisible Contributors)
2. Ensuring transparency to the extent possible
3. Disclosure of training use (when feasible)

### 11.2 Opt-Out
Creators may prohibit the use of their work for AI system training by clearly stating "Prohibited for AI Training" in the work or its accompanying documentation. This prohibition must be respected as an expression of the Creator's moral rights.

### 11.3 Secondary Use through AI Collaboration
When creating new works through AI collaboration based on This Work:
1. Follow the transparency requirements of this license
2. Provide proper attribution to the original work
3. Inherit respect for Invisible Contributors

## Article 12: Addressing Moral Rights Violations

### 12.1 Claims
When Creators believe their creative personality, honor, or reputation has been violated, they may file a claim with Users through:
1. Direct communication
2. Project issue systems
3. Other mutually agreed methods

### 12.2 Resolution Through Dialogue
1. Parties shall first attempt resolution through good faith dialogue
2. Community mediation may be sought when necessary
3. Efforts shall be made to understand differences in cultural and legal backgrounds

### 12.3 Remedial Measures
When moral rights violations are recognized, the following measures may be taken according to the situation:
1. Modification or removal of the offending portion
2. Correction of attribution
3. Public correction/apology (in serious cases)
4. Other measures agreed upon by the parties

### 12.4 Unintentional Violations
In cases of unintentional violations due to technical misunderstanding or cultural differences, opportunities for correction should be provided.
