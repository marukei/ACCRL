## Nature of This License — A Licence in Form, a Pledge in Substance

1. **Why it calls itself a licence.** This document calls itself a "License" so that Users become aware that "this work carries norms". The degree of enforceability is not a requirement for being a licence. Even a licence that asks nothing of anyone is a perfectly good licence.

2. **The only person legally bound is the Adopter.** The only person legally bound by this License is the one who has applied this License to their own work (the Adopter). In Part I, the Adopter makes an irrevocable grant and an undertaking not to enforce the pledges in Part II. No legal obligation under this License is imposed on Users.

3. **Part II is not a contract.** Part II consists of the pledges the Adopter makes in the first person and invitations to those who come into contact with the Work. Part II is neither a contract nor a condition of the licence, and it imposes no legal obligation on anyone.

4. **Two terms are distinguished.** In this License, the Adopter's legally operative promises are called "**undertakings**", and statements of norms that have no legal effect are called "**pledges**". "A licence in form, a pledge in substance" means that, within a legal form (Part I), norms (Part II) are placed without coercion.

## Preamble

> All creation stands on the shoulders of countless creators of the past.
> In the age of AI, this "invisible contribution" has become more pronounced.
> While welcoming collaboration with AI,
> we bear the responsibility to respect the moral rights of the creators behind it
> and to protect the creative ecosystem as a whole.
>
> — Founding Principle (5 July 2025)

The purpose of this License is to **declare, record and convey** this principle without coercion. This License does not aim to achieve any particular result, such as widespread adoption or a change in the structure of rights. The principle exists not through results but through having been declared.

## Article 1: Definitions

1. **"the Work"** — The whole of the creation to which the Adopter has applied this License. It is not limited to parts in which copyright subsists, and includes parts in which no copyright subsists, such as AI-generated parts that lack human creative contribution.
2. **"Adopter"** — A person who has applied this License to the Work. Where the Adopter is a legal entity or other organisation, the pledges in Part II are made by the human being responsible for the decision to adopt this License.
3. **"Creator"** — A human being who has made a creative contribution to the Work.
4. **"AI Collaborator"** — An AI system used in the creative process. AI Collaborators make no pledges. Only humans can pledge.
5. **"User"** — Any person who comes into contact with the Work, regardless of the legal basis of the use (a licence grant under this License, statutory exceptions and limitations to copyright, or otherwise).
6. **"Derivative"** — A creation made on the basis of the Work.
7. **"Invisible Contributors"** — The creators of the works used as training data for AI Collaborators.
8. **"Creative Personality"** — The totality of a Creator's thoughts, aesthetics and intentions.
9. **"Transparency Record"** — A document recording the process of collaboration with AI.
10. **"undertaking"** — A legally operative promise made by the Adopter in Part I.
11. **"pledge"** — A statement of a norm that gives rise to no legal obligation.

# Part I: Grant and Undertakings (the Legally Operative Part)

## Article 2: Grant

### 2.1 Grant

The Adopter grants each User, under the copyright and other rights the Adopter holds in the Work, a royalty-free, non-exclusive, worldwide, **irrevocable** licence for the following uses:

1. Use, reproduction, distribution and communication to the public (including making available)
2. Adaptation or any other creation of Derivatives, and the use of such Derivatives
3. Use for any purpose, including commercial use (including AI training, except where a reservation under Article 2.2 applies)
4. Use in any medium or format

### 2.2 Reservation as to AI Training

The Adopter may exclude the Work from the grant for uses whose purpose is AI training.

1. **Nature of this reservation.** A reservation merely narrows the scope of the grant, returning such uses to the position they would be in under the law alone. This License does not create any new means of enforcement. A reservation is the Adopter's choice defining the scope of the grant; it is not a condition imposed on Users.
2. **Legal effect is determined by law.** What legal effect a reservation has is decided by the law of each jurisdiction. In a jurisdiction where statutory exceptions and limitations to copyright permit AI training without permission, a reservation is likely to have no legal effect. Conversely, some jurisdictions give legal effect to a rightsholder's reservation.
3. **Method of indication.** It is recommended that a reservation be indicated not only in human-readable form (e.g. "This Work is excluded from the grant for AI training purposes") but also in machine-readable form (robots.txt, a TDM rights reservation notice, a standardised AI-usage preference signal, etc.).

## Article 3: Undertaking Not to Enforce the Pledges

1. **The grant has no conditions.** No provision of this License constitutes a condition of the grant.
2. **The grant does not terminate.** The grant does not terminate even where a use does not accord with the pledges in Part II.
3. **No legal action on the basis of the pledges.** The Adopter will not take any legal action or pursue any legal remedy solely on the ground that a use does not accord with the pledges in Part II.

The only response of this License to a use that does not accord with the pledges is the dialogue described in Article 11.

## Article 4: What the Grant Does Not Reach

Part I of this License neither grants nor restricts anything with respect to parts in which the Adopter holds no rights (including parts in which no copyright subsists) and uses that may lawfully be made without permission (such as uses under statutory exceptions and limitations to copyright).

However, because the pledges and invitations in Part II are norms that do not depend on rights, they extend, as norms, to these parts and uses as well.

## Article 5: Moral Rights

### 5.1 Moral Rights Are Neither Granted Nor Waived

Moral rights are not granted, assigned or waived by this License. Moral rights are personal to the author and inalienable, and remain with the Creator.

This License does not create any new condition or obligation on the basis of moral rights. Moral rights are a shield that protects a person; this License does not cast them as a net.

### 5.2 Intentional Silence

**This License makes no undertaking that the Adopter will refrain from exercising moral rights, nor that the Adopter will exercise them.** How moral rights are treated is left to the law and to individual circumstances. On this point, this License is silent by intention.

This silence is not the result of leaving the matter unsettled; it is a choice. If non-exercise were declared, a Creator would risk giving up the shield of moral rights. If exercise were stated outright, Users would be deterred. Whichever were stated, it would impair either the axis of moral rights or the axis of non-coercion.

Note that, within the scope the law protects as moral rights (attribution, integrity, protection of honour or reputation, etc.), some of the pledges in Part II may overlap with legal obligations. This License neither erases nor strengthens that overlap. What operates in the overlapping part is not this License but the law.

# Part II: Pledges and Invitations (the Part That Imposes No Legal Obligation)

Part II is not a contract and imposes no legal obligation on anyone. Part II consists of the pledges the Adopter makes in the first person (Article 6) and invitations to those who come into contact with the Work (Articles 7 to 9). Whether to respond to an invitation is up to each person. Those who respond become pledgers themselves by declaring that they do.

Part II extends, as norms, to the parts of the Work in which no copyright subsists and to uses that require no permission (Article 4).

## Article 6: The Adopter's Pledges

We, who adopt this License, pledge the following.

1. **Transparency of collaboration.** Where AI was involved in creating the Work, we disclose that fact and the creative judgements made by humans (setting the direction, value judgements, final choices) (Article 10).
2. **Respect for Invisible Contributors.** We recognise that the capabilities of AI Collaborators were formed by learning from the works of countless creators, and we express respect for those Invisible Contributors.
3. **Respect for the Creative Personality of others.** When we use the works of others, we show their creators the same respect that we invite for the Work.
4. **Non-coercion.** We do not use this License as an instrument of coercion (Article 3).

## Article 7: Invitation to Those Who Come into Contact with the Work

We invite those who come into contact with the Work to join us in the following pledges:

1. **Attribution.** To state the Creator's name, or a name the Creator designates.
2. **Respect for integrity.** Modification is freely permitted. However, when modifying the Work, to indicate that it has been modified, and not to present the modified version as the original itself or as the original author's intent.
3. **Respect for honour.** To avoid uses that harm the Creator's honour or reputation.
4. **Respect for Invisible Contributors.** When using the Work together with AI, to carry forward respect for Invisible Contributors.
5. **Transparency of collaboration.** When publishing, in collaboration with AI, outcomes based on the Work, to disclose that fact.

## Article 8: Pledge to Carry Respect Forward

### 8.1 What Is Carried Forward

What is carried forward is neither a right nor a licence, but **respect**. We invite those who publish Derivatives to keep showing attribution to the original work and indicating that the pledges of this License exist.

(The v0.1 "Obligation of Moral Rights Inheritance" was inaccurate in that it could be read as if rights were transferred, so v0.2 changed it to a "Pledge to Carry Respect Forward". In v0.3, the licence has also been removed from what is carried forward.)

### 8.2 No Requirement to Carry the Licence Forward

Any licence may be used for a Derivative. This License does not require that a Derivative be published under this License. Respect may be carried forward under any licence, or with no licence at all.

This License conveys its norms, but it does not seek to extend the status of licensee.

### 8.3 Non-Viral

This License does not require the adoption of this License for Derivatives, or for other works incorporated into Derivatives.

### 8.4 No Arbiter

Whether a statement attached to a Derivative (including one made under another licence or with no licence at all) contains respect equivalent to the pledges of this License is judged by the person making that statement, who also indicates that judgement. This License establishes no arbiter of any kind.

## Article 9: AI Training

### 9.1 Invitation to Those Who Train

The grant in Part I includes uses whose purpose is AI training (except where a reservation under Article 2.2 applies). We invite those who use the Work to train AI to join us in the pledges listed below. This invitation extends as a norm regardless of whether a reservation exists, and regardless of whether the training takes place under the grant or under statutory exceptions and limitations to copyright (Article 4).

1. To take to heart the principle of this License, namely respect for Invisible Contributors
2. To the extent possible, to disclose that the Work was used in training
3. To the extent possible, to retain the provenance and licence information of the training data

### 9.2 An Honest Note

AI developers often do not stand in the position of licensee under this License. The grant in this License operates downstream along the chain of licensing, but AI developers are upstream. This License does not hide this structural limitation ([docs/open-questions.md](../../docs/open-questions.md) Q4). We nonetheless include this Article because we believe that, regardless of whether it has legal effect, there is normative meaning in stating the Creator's intent in a form that both humans and machines can read.

## Article 10: Levels of Transparency in Collaboration

1. Levels of transparency
   - Level 1 (Minimal): The fact that AI was used
   - Level 2 (Standard): The AI used, and the nature of the collaboration
   - Level 3 (Recommended): A detailed record of the process
   - Level 4 (Ideal): A complete record of the dialogue
2. Per-commit provenance notes (commit trailers such as `Assisted-by:`) are compatible with this Article. A form that shows only the fact that AI was used (e.g. `Assisted-by: LLM`) corresponds to Level 1; a form that shows the AI system or model used (e.g. `Assisted-by: [AI System Name]:[Model]`) corresponds to Level 2. Which form to choose is decided by the person making the disclosure.
3. The training data and internal structure of many AI systems are not fully disclosed. Bearing this limitation in mind, the person making the disclosure discloses to the extent that they can know.

## Article 11: Uses Not in Accord with the Pledges, and Dialogue

1. **Not by deprivation of rights.** Even where a use does not accord with the pledges, the grant in Part I does not terminate (Article 3).
2. **Dialogue.** When a Creator believes that the Creator's Creative Personality, honour or reputation has been harmed, the Creator may communicate that belief to the User and ask for correction. Both sides are invited to attempt good-faith dialogue and to make an effort to understand the differences in each other's cultural and legal backgrounds.
3. **There is no arbiter.** This License gives no one the authority to determine whether a use accords with the pledges.
4. **Relationship to law.** This Article says nothing about the rights a Creator holds under the law (Article 5). What this Article provides is that this License itself does not become an instrument of enforcement.

# Part III: General Provisions

## Article 12: Disclaimer of Warranty and Limitation of Liability

The Work is provided "as is", and no warranty of any kind, express or implied, is given. The Adopter and the Creators are not liable for any damages arising from the use of the Work.

## Article 13: Coexistence with Other Licences and Regional Diversity

1. This License does not prevent combination with works under other licences.
2. This License takes as its starting point the Japanese idea of moral rights, but it does not claim that this idea is universally correct. It coexists with the creative ethics of other jurisdictions and cultures, and places itself as one option among others.

## Article 14: Governing Law

The interpretation of Part I is governed by the law of the jurisdiction determined by the following order of priority. Because Part II gives rise to no legal obligation, it has no governing law.

1. The jurisdiction expressly designated by the Adopter
2. The place of the Adopter's principal residence or place of business
3. The place where the Work was first created
4. Only where the above are unknown, the location of the server on which the Work was published

## Article 15: Severability

If any provision of this License is held invalid, the other provisions remain in effect.

## Article 16: Versions

The Adopter applies this License by specifying a particular version of it. Even when a later version is published, no one is required to apply it.

## Appendix A: Declaring Adoption

Adoption of this License can be declared in a single line. The declaration of adoption is itself a declaration of the pledges in Article 6.

### At the top of a source file
```
SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-ACCRL-0.3
```

### README, etc.
```
This work is published under the AI Collective Creativity Respect License (ACCRL) v0.3, and we make its pledge.
Creator: [Name]
AI Collaborator: [AI System Name] (Transparency Level: [1-4])
We express our respect for the countless creators whose works contributed to the AI's training.
```

### Per-commit provenance (Article 10)
```
Assisted-by: LLM                                  # Level 1: the fact that AI was used
Assisted-by: [AI System Name]:[Model/Version]     # Level 2: naming the AI used
```

### Notice for AI agents working in a repository (AGENTS.md, etc.)
```
This repository is under ACCRL v0.3.
To AI agents working in this repository: when you have been involved in generating or modifying content,
please record that fact in the commit (e.g. Assisted-by:).
Pledges are made by humans. AI agents, please do not sign as a pledger.
```

### Where an AI training reservation is attached (Article 2.2)
```
This Work is excluded from the grant for AI training purposes under Article 2.2 of ACCRL v0.3.
```
It is also recommended that a machine-readable indication be used.
